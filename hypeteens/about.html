About HYPETEENS

Discover who we are and what we stand for.

Who We Are

HYPETEENS is a community of young people united by a common desire to learn, grow, develop and make a positive impact in society. We believe that young people have the potential to become responsible leaders and agents of change.

Our History

HYPETEENS was established to bring young people together, encourage positive development and create opportunities for growth and collaboration.

Our Vision

To build a generation of empowered, responsible and visionary young people who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate and empower young people through meaningful activities, education, leadership development and community engagement.

Our Objectives

01. Education

Encourage learning, knowledge sharing and personal development.

02. Leadership

Develop responsible, confident and visionary young leaders.

03. Empowerment

Promote skills development and opportunities for young people.

04. Community

Encourage teamwork, volunteerism and positive community impact.

Our Core Values

Integrity Teamwork Respect Discipline Commitment Excellence
